\abstrak

Kabupaten Sukabumi memiliki aktivitas pertanian dan distribusi komoditas pangan yang cukup besar, sehingga pemantauan harga bahan pangan menjadi salah satu aspek penting dalam mendukung stabilitas harga di daerah. Komoditas pangan memiliki karakteristik harga yang dinamis dan cenderung berfluktuasi, sehingga informasi mengenai harga aktual saja belum cukup untuk mendukung proses pemantauan dan pengambilan keputusan. Berdasarkan kondisi tersebut, diperlukan suatu sistem yang dapat mengintegrasikan pengumpulan data harga komoditas dan data pendukung dari instansi terkait serta menyediakan informasi prediksi harga berdasarkan data historis. Solusi yang ditawarkan adalah pengembangan Sistem Informasi Pemantauan Harga Komoditas (SIPANTAU) berbasis web yang menyediakan fungsi pengelolaan data komoditas serta penyajian hasil prediksi harga komoditas untuk tiga hari ke depan sebagai informasi pendukung bagi Tim Pengendali Inflasi Daerah (TPID).

Pengembangan SIPANTAU pada kegiatan Praktik Kerja Lapang difokuskan pada pembangunan \textit{backend} menggunakan \textit{framework} Laravel, pengelolaan basis data MySQL, penerapan \textit{authentication} dan \textit{authorization} berbasis \textit{role}, integrasi layanan model prediksi, serta \textit{deployment} pada lingkungan produksi. Metode pelaksanaan dilakukan melalui tahapan analisis kondisi dan kebutuhan sistem, perancangan basis data dan arsitektur aplikasi, implementasi, pengujian, serta \textit{deployment} dan pengujian pada lingkungan produksi. \textit{Backend} Laravel digunakan untuk mengelola data komoditas, pasar, ketersediaan, panen, harga petani, dan harga pasar melalui API, sedangkan proses prediksi dilakukan melalui layanan FastAPI yang menerima data historis harga pasar, ketersediaan harian, kebutuhan harian, dan neraca harian untuk menghasilkan prediksi harga tiga hari ke depan. Hasil pengujian menunjukkan bahwa fungsi pengelolaan data, \textit{authentication}, \textit{authorization}, dan prediksi dapat berjalan sesuai dengan rancangan, serta seluruh komponen aplikasi berhasil diterapkan pada lingkungan produksi dan diakses melalui HTTPS. Hasil pembahasan menunjukkan bahwa pengembangan telah memenuhi kebutuhan utama sistem dalam pengelolaan data dan penyediaan hasil prediksi harga. Pengembangan selanjutnya diarahkan pada peningkatan pengelolaan data, pengelolaan akun dan hak akses, pelengkapan data masukan prediksi, serta integrasi sumber data eksternal seperti SILINDA.

\vspace{1cm}

\noindent\textbf{Kata kunci:} SIPANTAU, website, \textit{backend}, Laravel, MySQL, Sukabumi, komoditas

\abstraken

Sukabumi Regency has substantial agricultural activities and food commodity distribution, making food price monitoring an important aspect of supporting price stability in the region. Food commodities are characterized by dynamic and fluctuating prices, making actual price information alone insufficient to support monitoring and decision-making processes. Based on these conditions, a system is needed to integrate the collection of commodity price data and supporting data from relevant agencies while providing price prediction information based on historical data. The proposed solution is the development of the Commodity Price Monitoring Information System (SIPANTAU), a web-based application that provides commodity data management and presents commodity price predictions for the next three days as supporting information for the Regional Inflation Control Team (TPID).

The development of SIPANTAU during the Practical Work focused on developing the \textit{backend} using the Laravel \textit{framework}, managing the MySQL database, implementing role-based \textit{authentication} and \textit{authorization}, integrating the prediction model service, and \textit{deploying} the application in a production environment. The implementation method consisted of analyzing system conditions and requirements, designing the database and application architecture, implementation, testing, and \textit{deployment} and testing in the production environment. The Laravel \textit{backend} was used to manage commodity, market, availability, harvest, farmer price, and market price data through an API, while the prediction process was performed through a FastAPI service that receives historical data on market prices, daily availability, daily demand, and daily balance to generate price predictions for the next three days. The test results showed that the data management, \textit{authentication}, \textit{authorization}, and prediction functions operated according to the design, and all application components were successfully deployed in the production environment and accessed through HTTPS. The discussion results showed that the development fulfilled the main system requirements for data management and the provision of price prediction results. Further development is directed toward improving data management, account and access-right management, completing prediction input data, and integrating external data sources such as SILINDA.

\vspace{1cm}
